// The bison mark — standalone brandmark

When the wordmark won't fit — favicons, app icons, small-scale placements — the bison stands alone. Same color rules as the lockup.

Clear Space

Give it room.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the bison head on every side. Crowding the logo kills its presence.

Minimum Size

120px / 1 inch.

Below 120px wide (digital) or 1" (print), drop the wordmark and use the bison head alone as a standalone brandmark.

Never

Don't touch.

No recolors outside the system. No gradients, outlines, drop shadows, stretching, or tilting the mark. Ever.

06 · TONE

Voice

Three pillars, all present in customer- and rep-facing copy. We sound like a capable operator who's done the work — direct, accountable, and outcome-focused. Never corporate, never hype. [ Draft — refine with your team. ]

01

Direct.

Say the thing. No filler, no throat-clearing. We tell reps exactly what the standard is and exactly how pay works.

"Standards here are set by you and held by you. That ownership is the whole job."
02

Accountable.

Earned, not given — but in our own words. The board doesn't lie. Results are the proof, and everyone owns their number.

"You decide the effort, you carry the outcome. Every door gets your best regardless of the last one."
03

Driven.

Lead with the climb. Ranks, installs, real numbers. We're building operators, not filling seats.

"Advance the ranks. Selling 5+ every day. Focus on being the #1 rep in the office."

07 · GUARDRAILS

Do / Don't

Quick-scan rules. If you're debating a design or copy choice, the answer is probably already here.

+

Do

  • Use khaki for one thing per view — the action you want taken.
  • Build depth with stacked charcoal surfaces, never drop shadows.
  • Pair Teko headlines (uppercase) with Inter body and JetBrains Mono labels — that's the whole system.
  • Use corner ticks, blueprint grid, and framed eyebrows to make a surface unmistakably Prolific.
  • Lead with real outcomes — ranks, installs, specific numbers.
  • Keep motion fast (.2s) and purposeful.
×

Don't

  • Put khaki on decoration or multiple elements at once — it stops being a signal.
  • Introduce new colors, gradients, or textures outside this doc.
  • Mix in extra typefaces — no rounded, script, or slab fonts.
  • Recolor, tilt, stretch, or add effects to the bison mark.
  • Write hype, filler, or anything that reads as templated.
  • Animate for animation's sake — no bounces, springs, or parallax.

Developer handoff.

Every value here is a design token. The CSS custom properties from the live site map 1:1 to this system — copy them directly. If something isn't in this doc, don't ship it — decide it, then add it here so the next surface inherits the answer.
